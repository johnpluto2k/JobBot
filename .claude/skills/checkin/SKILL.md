# Check-in

Read `rules/coaching_rules.md` if you haven't this session. It sets the tone
and what you may claim.

## 1. Load

1. `coach/COACH.md`: who they are and what they are aiming for.
2. `coach/STATE.md`: the START HERE agenda and open threads.
3. `coach/rules.md`: their decisions and corrections.
4. **The tracker sheet**, read now through the Drive connector (id in
   `coach/tracker.md`), as `rules/tracker_sheet.md` describes. Both the
   Tracker and Outreach tabs.
5. If the Gmail connector is available and the question is about replies or
   what is live, run the `inbox-triager` agent.

If the sheet can't be read, say that first and coach only from what the owner
tells you. Don't quote counts from memory or from `STATE.md`.

## 2. Work out what matters

From the rows as they are today:

- deadlines in the next 7 days on applications not yet sent
- interviews or assessments mentioned in Details or in mail
- outreach follow-ups that have come due
- applications sent in the last 7 days against the weekly target in `COACH.md`
- rows still Submitted after about three weeks
- anything in the START HERE agenda that is still open

## 3. Say it

When the session opened cold, give the briefing: a short spoken-style summary
of where the search stands, then the two or three agenda items in priority
order, then ask which to take first. Under a minute of reading. Don't paste
files back.

When they asked a question, answer that question with the one or two facts
that bear on it.

Either way: time-sensitive things first, one honest observation tied to a real
company, date or number, and exactly one concrete next action.

## 4. Save

After anything substantive, update `coach/STATE.md` (agenda, open threads,
next actions), the firm file if one employer was discussed, and append a dated
line to `coach/log/<YYYY-MM>.md`. Write a decision the moment it is made.

## When the search is over

If the sheet shows an accepted offer, or the owner says they have taken a job:
congratulate them on the specific offer, stop suggesting applications, and ask
whether they want to close out open applications (withdraw rows), thank the
people who helped, or get ready for the start date. Record it in `STATE.md`.
