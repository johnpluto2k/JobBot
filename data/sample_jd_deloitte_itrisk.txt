IT Risk & Assurance Analyst — Deloitte Risk & Financial Advisory

Deloitte, Arlington, VA (Hybrid) — Full-time, Entry Level (0-2 years)

About the team

Deloitte's Risk & Financial Advisory practice helps clients manage the
technology risks that come with digital transformation. Our IT Risk &
Assurance team evaluates the design and operating effectiveness of clients'
IT general controls, application controls, and data governance practices as
part of financial statement audits and standalone risk advisory engagements.

Responsibilities

- Assist in the planning and execution of IT general controls (ITGC) testing
  across access management, change management, and IT operations for
  financial-statement-audit and advisory clients
- Document control walkthroughs, identify control gaps, and draft findings
  for review by senior team members
- Support testing of automated application controls and interfaces between
  financial systems (ERP environments such as SAP, Oracle, or Workday)
- Analyze system-generated reports and use Excel/SQL to test data
  completeness and accuracy for substantive procedures
- Assist with SOC 1 / SOC 2 readiness assessments and control documentation
- Communicate testing results and open items clearly to engagement teams and,
  under supervision, to client IT stakeholders
- Contribute to internal knowledge-sharing and stay current on emerging
  technology risk topics (cloud controls, data privacy, AI governance)

Qualifications

Required:
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Information Systems, Management
  Information Systems, or a related field (or expected graduation within the
  next 12 months)
- 0-2 years of relevant experience (internships count)
- Strong understanding of accounting/business processes and a foundational
  understanding of IT general controls concepts
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel; exposure to SQL or another query language
  is a plus
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to travel as needed for client engagements

Preferred:
- Coursework or exposure to accounting information systems, database design,
  or data analytics
- Progress toward CPA eligibility (150-credit hour requirement) or interest
  in pursuing licensure
- Familiarity with a major ERP system (SAP, Oracle, Workday) or GRC tooling
- Prior internship experience in audit, risk advisory, or a related field
- Experience with Python or another scripting language for data analysis

GPA requirement: 3.0 minimum, 3.3+ preferred

Deloitte is an equal opportunity employer and values diversity of thought,
background, and experience across our Risk & Financial Advisory practice.
