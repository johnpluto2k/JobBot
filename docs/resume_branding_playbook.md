# Resume & Professional Branding Playbook (John Bae Standard)

## Core Philosophy

-   Never lie or invent experience
-   Expand real responsibilities using stronger business language
-   Preserve accuracy while improving impact

Example:

**Weak** \> Managed resident database

**Strong** \> Managed compliance-ready lease, payment, & regulatory
records for **800+ tenants**, strengthening documentation accuracy &
audit preparedness

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Resume Bullet Formula

Every bullet follows this structure:

**Action Verb → What You Did → How You Did It → Business Impact**

Always answer: 1. What did you do? 2. How did you do it? 3. Why did it
matter? 4. Can it be quantified?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Bullet Length Rules

## Short Bullets

-   **85--95 characters**

Example: \> Led weekly technical sessions for **28 students**,
strengthening analytical reasoning

## Long Bullets

-   **170--190 characters**

Example: \> Managed compliance-ready lease, payment, & regulatory
records for **800+ tenants**, strengthening internal controls,
documentation accuracy, & audit preparedness

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Formatting Rules

-   No periods at the end of bullets
-   Use "&" instead of "and"
-   Bold all metrics
-   Keep verb tense consistent
-   Maintain clean spacing & formatting

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Metrics

Always bold measurable results.

Examples: - **800+ tenants** - **15%** - **\$380K** - **\$25K** - **100+
tax returns** - **20% reduction**

Recruiters naturally scan numbers first.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Preferred Action Verbs

Avoid: - Helped - Worked on - Responsible for - Assisted

Use: - Managed - Conducted - Analyzed - Led - Prepared - Reviewed -
Developed - Strengthened - Leveraged - Implemented - Optimized -
Coordinated - Evaluated

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# ATS Optimization

Naturally incorporate keywords from each job description.

For Big 4 Tax & Advisory: - Audit - Tax - Compliance - Internal
Controls - Financial Reporting - Documentation - Client Service - Data
Analytics - Excel - Power BI - AI - Risk - Governance

Avoid keyword stuffing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Resume Tailoring Strategy

Keep: - **80--90%** of the resume the same

Tailor: - **10--20%** of wording - Keywords - Skills ordering -
Leadership emphasis - Company-specific language

Never rewrite the entire resume unnecessarily.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Company Research

Before tailoring: - Read the mission - Read the values - Read the job
description - Understand leadership principles

Subtly mirror company language.

Examples:

-   Integrity
-   Innovation
-   Quality
-   Future-forward
-   Client service

## Deloitte

-   Impact
-   Leadership
-   Trust
-   Innovation

## PwC

-   Solve important problems
-   Technology
-   Collaboration

## EY

-   Building a better working world
-   Transformation
-   Analytics

Do not copy slogans directly.

# Cover Letter Structure

## Paragraph 1

-   Who you are
-   Why this company
-   One unique reason you're interested

## Paragraph 2

Highlight: - Tax - Leadership - Analytics - Client service - Technology

## Paragraph 3

Mention: - Coursework - CPA eligibility - Relevant experiences -
Interest in the role

## Paragraph 4

Short, appreciative closing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Recruiter Emails

Keep to **5--8 sentences**.

Structure: 1. Greeting 2. Hope you're doing well 3. Purpose 4. Continued
interest 5. Thank them 6. Signature

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Networking Emails

Always: - Thank them - Reference previous interaction - Explain why
you're reaching out - Stay under **150 words** - Never ask directly for
a job - Ask about opportunities or next steps

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# LinkedIn Strategy

## Headline

Example:

> Accounting & Information Science Student at the University of Maryland
> \| CPA Candidate \| Tax \| Data Analytics

## About Section

Tell a story: - Why accounting - Why information science - Career
goals - What you're seeking

## Experience

Resume: - \~3 bullets

LinkedIn: - \~5--7 bullets

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Personal Brand

Core positioning:

> Accounting student with a strong data analytics background who
> combines technical skills with client service, compliance, & business
> problem-solving

Position yourself at the intersection of: - Accounting - Technology -
Analytics

This aligns well with Big 4 Tax, Advisory, & Consulting opportunities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The John Bae Standard

Every bullet should answer:

1.  What did you do?
2.  How did you do it?
3.  Why did it matter?
4.  Can you quantify it?

If it answers at least three of these well, it's likely a strong resume
bullet.
