%   - Exactly one page, with 0.20-0.35in of blank space at the bottom.
%   - Every bullet is ONE line that ends 92-99% of the way to the right

%   - Every bullet starts with a different action verb.
%   - 3 bullets per job, 3 per project, 2 per leadership entry. Fill the

%   - Plain ASCII: write "resume" and "cafe" without accents, and use " -- "
