Data Analyst, Operations — Northwind

Northwind, Chicago, IL (Hybrid) — Full-time, Entry Level (0-2 years)

About the team

Northwind's Operations Analytics team turns warehouse, delivery and customer
data into the reports and models the business runs on. You will work with
operations managers and data engineers to find where time and money are lost
and show what to change.

Responsibilities

- Write SQL against the data warehouse to build and maintain weekly operations
  reporting
- Build and maintain Tableau dashboards used by regional managers
- Clean and validate data from several source systems and document data
  quality rules
- Use Python (pandas) to automate recurring analyses and reduce manual
  reporting work
- Design and read A/B tests on routing and staffing changes
- Present findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders

Qualifications

Required:
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science, Economics, or a related
  field (or expected graduation within the next 12 months)
- 0-2 years of relevant experience (internships count)
- Proficiency in SQL and Excel
- Experience with a data visualization tool such as Tableau or Power BI
- Strong written and verbal communication

Preferred:
- Python or R for data analysis
- Familiarity with regression and A/B testing
- Experience with PostgreSQL or another relational database
- Exposure to forecasting or machine learning
