Data Analyst, Network Planning (New Graduate)
Northwind Logistics -- Chicago, IL (hybrid, 3 days a week in office)
Start date: Summer 2027
Posting ID: NWL-2027-0142

About Northwind Logistics
Northwind Logistics is a regional freight carrier moving less-than-truckload
shipments across nine Midwestern states. Our Network Planning team decides
where freight goes, which carriers move it, and how many people each
warehouse needs on each shift.

About the role
You will join Network Planning as a full-time Data Analyst. Planners will
come to you with questions such as "why is this region missing its delivery
windows?" and you will find the answer in our shipment data, explain it
plainly, and build the report that keeps the question answered.

What you will do
- Write SQL against our shipment, carrier, and warehouse staffing databases
  to answer planners' questions about on-time delivery and cost.
- Build and maintain Tableau dashboards used in the weekly network review.
- Help forecast weekly freight volume by region so warehouses can staff ahead.
- Automate recurring reports and reconciliations in Python or Excel.
- Check data quality: find duplicate, missing, or mismatched records and work
  with the source teams to fix them.
- Present findings to planners and operations managers who are not analysts.

What we are looking for
- Bachelor's degree by June 2027 in Information Systems, Analytics,
  Statistics, Supply Chain, Industrial Engineering, or a related field.
- Working SQL: joins, aggregations, and window functions.
- Experience with a dashboard tool (Tableau preferred; Power BI is fine).
- Comfort in Excel, including PivotTables and lookups.
- Clear writing and the ability to explain a number to someone in operations.

Nice to have
- Python (pandas) for data cleaning or automation.
- Coursework or a project in forecasting or operations management.
- An internship or part-time job in logistics, retail, or warehouse operations.

Pay and benefits
$62,000 - $70,000 a year, depending on experience. Health, dental, and vision
insurance; 401(k) with company match; 15 days of paid time off.

How to apply
Submit a one-page resume and a cover letter at careers.northwind-logistics.example.
Applications are reviewed in the order they arrive.

Northwind Logistics is an equal opportunity employer.
